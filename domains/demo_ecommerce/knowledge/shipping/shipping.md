# Shipping Policy

## Standard shipping

- Standard shipping costs $5.
- Estimated delivery time is 3–5 business days.
- Delivery estimates begin after the order is confirmed.

## Express shipping

- Express shipping costs $12.
- Estimated delivery time is 1–2 business days.
- Express delivery is available only for eligible destinations.

## Delivery estimates

Delivery estimates are estimates, not guarantees. Delays can occur because of carrier or destination conditions.

## Order status

This document does not contain live order status. Never invent an order's current location, dispatch status, or delivery date. Use the order lookup tool when live order information is required.
