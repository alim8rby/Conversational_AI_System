# Returns Policy

## Return window

- Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- The item should be unused and in its original condition.
- Original packaging should be included when reasonably possible.

## Refunds

- Approved returns are refunded to the original payment method.
- Refund processing begins after the returned item is received and inspected.
- The refund may take additional time to appear depending on the payment provider.

## Non-returnable condition

Items that have been used, damaged by the customer, or returned outside the stated window may be ineligible for a return.

## Return requests

This policy explains eligibility but does not submit a return request. Use the return workflow when a customer wants to initiate a return.
