# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I check whether a product is in stock?

The catalog does not provide live inventory. A product search or inventory tool must be used before confirming current availability.

## Can I track my order?

Yes. Live order status requires the order lookup tool. Do not infer or invent the status from the customer's description.

## How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping is estimated at 3–5 business days. Express shipping is estimated at 1–2 business days for eligible destinations.

## Can I return an item?

A return may be requested within 30 calendar days of delivery if the item meets the return conditions in the returns policy.

## What if I have a problem that the knowledge base cannot answer?

Do not invent an answer. Explain that the available information is insufficient and route the request to the appropriate support workflow or escalation path.
